# Creative targets — big children's brands (manual outreach only)

`src/lib/prospect-targets.ts` has always pointed at "the separate creative-targets
file" for the big children's brands with no cold-emailable address. This is that
file. None of these belong in the automated invite pipeline: they are reached
through corporate giving portals, PR teams, or a warm introduction (often via
Lurie corporate/foundation relations), and a gold-foil cold email to a generic
inbox would go nowhere.

Ronald McDonald House Charities Chicagoland & Northwest Indiana is NOT here —
it has a published address (info@rmhccni.org) and lives in the email pipeline.

| Org | Angle | Channel | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Abbott (Abbott Fund) | Chicagoland healthcare giant; nutrition (Similac) + community giving | https://www.abbott.com/responsibility.html — corporate giving portal; better via Lurie corporate-dev | Higher-dollar sponsorship or grant; expect a long lead. |
| Mead Johnson / Enfamil (Reckitt) | Infant nutrition; pediatric families | Corporate contact page; PR/CSR team | Brand fit with a children's-hospital conference; needs a person, not a portal, to land. |
| Medela | Breastfeeding/lactation products; NICU families | https://www.medela.us — corporate/CSR contact | Chicagoland HQ (McHenry); genuine pediatric fit. |
| WTTW / PBS Kids Chicago | Chicago public media; bilingual children's programming | https://www.wttw.com — partnerships/development office | Media partnership rather than paid sponsorship; pairs with the Univision/Telemundo media asks in the manual doc. |

When one of these lands a named contact with a real email, either handle it
personally end-to-end or move it into `prospect-targets.ts` with an
individually written note.
