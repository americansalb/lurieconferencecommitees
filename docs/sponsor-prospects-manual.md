# Sponsor prospects — manual outreach (FORM + WARM)

These prospects came from the 2026 sponsor-prospects research list. They are **not**
in the automated invite pipeline (`src/lib/prospect-targets.ts`) because they have
**no emailable address** — they are reached through a web contact form (FORM) or
must be routed warmly through Lurie development (WARM). The emailable rows from the
same research live in `prospect-targets.ts` and load via **Sponsors → Load suggested
targets**.

Work these by hand. Duplicates already merged into the email list (CASL, Erie,
Indo-American, UnidosUS, Pocketalk) are not repeated here. Health Nuts Media was held
out of the email list pending verification of an aggregator-sourced address.
